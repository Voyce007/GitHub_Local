# Group Glossary (wiki export)

Exported 2024-09-02 from the group wiki. Page owner: Group Data Office.

- **Customer**: A person or organisation that buys, or has applied to buy, from any company in the group. Synonyms: Client, Account, Debtor.
- **Party**: A person or organisation the group has a relationship with.
- **Customer Number**: The group-wide identifier for a customer, used on every invoice. Synonyms: Customer ID, Debtor Number.
- **Product**: Something the group offers for sale, whether goods or services.
- **Order**: A customer's request to buy one or more products.
- **Service Contract**: An agreement to maintain a customer's machines for a period.
- **Invoice**: A request for payment issued by a legal entity to a customer.
- **Credit Limit**: The most the group will let a customer owe across all group companies.
- **Exposure**: The total a customer owes the group, plus accepted orders not yet invoiced.
- **Legal Entity**: A registered company within the group that can trade and raise invoices.
- **Cost Centre**: A unit of the organisation to which costs are assigned for management reporting.
- **Risk**: An uncertain event that could affect the group's objectives.
- **Control**: An activity that reduces the likelihood or impact of a risk.
