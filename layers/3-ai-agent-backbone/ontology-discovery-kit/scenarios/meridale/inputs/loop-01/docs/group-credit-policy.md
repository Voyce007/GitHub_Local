# Group Credit Policy (extract)

Version 4.1 · March 2024 · Group Finance

## Scope

Applies to every customer of every operating company and every legal entity in the group.
Credit limits are set per customer at group level, not per operating company.

## Rules

- Total exposure to a customer must be measured across all legal entities in the group.
- A customer whose credit status is Stopped cannot place new orders in any operating company.
- Credit limits above 250,000 GBP must be approved by the Group Credit Controller.

## Classifications

Credit status values: Approved, On Hold, Stopped.

## Identifiers

The Customer Number is issued by FinCore when the customer is first approved for credit.
