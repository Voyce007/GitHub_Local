# SME answers to discovery loop 1 questions

Recorded 2026-12-10 in a working session with the Group Financial Controller, the Group Credit Controller, the Sales Operations lead and the Head of Enterprise Architecture.

## Customer identity

The Customer Number is issued by FinCore when the customer is first approved for credit. TradeDesk and ServiceLink store it once FinCore returns it.

"Client" is a synonym of Customer.

"Account" is not a synonym of Customer; in FinCore it means a general ledger account.

"OrderRequest" is a synonym of Order.

## Credit

Credit status values: Approved, Provisional, On Hold, Stopped.

## Orders

Order status values: Basket, Placed, Paid, Shipped, Delivered, Cancelled.

The 2019 order-to-cash overview pre-dates the 2022 web store rebuild and should be archived.

## Systems

CreditScore (also called credit-score-svc) calculates an external credit score for every new customer.

## Still open

Exposure is not held in any system today. Finance and Sales still need to agree how accepted orders that are not yet invoiced should count.
