# Childcare Scheme Guide (staff extract)

Version 2.1 · May 2024 · Family Support

## Overview

ChildcarePortal (also called the parent account) captures and fulfils parent payments to childcare providers.
Eligibility depends on each parent's expected income, which is taken from their PAYE employment records.

## Rules

- A parent must reconfirm eligibility every three months.
- A parent whose expected income exceeds 100,000 is not eligible.
- The top-up must be paid within one working day of the parent payment.
- A childcare account must be closed when the account holder dies.

## Classifications

Childcare account status values: Open, Suspended, Closed.
