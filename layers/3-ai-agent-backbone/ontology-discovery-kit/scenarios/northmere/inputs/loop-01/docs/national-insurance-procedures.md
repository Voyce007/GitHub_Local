# National Insurance Procedures (extract)

Version 3.4 · October 2023 · NI Operations

## Overview

NumberHub (also called NI allocation) allocates National Insurance numbers. It replaced the TAXCORE allocation module in 2022.
Class 1 contributions are calculated in TAXCORE from the earnings in each employer return.

## Identifiers

The National Insurance Number is issued by NumberHub when a person reaches 16.

## Rules

- A person must have exactly one National Insurance Number for life.
- A tax year counts as a qualifying year when contributions reach the lower earnings limit.
- Contributions must not be calculated for a person after their recorded date of death.

## Classifications

NI category values: A, B, C, H, J, M, Z.
