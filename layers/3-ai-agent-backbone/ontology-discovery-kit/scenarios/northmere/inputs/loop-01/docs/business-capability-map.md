# Business Capability Map (level 2 extract)

Version 1.3 · June 2025 · Enterprise Architecture

Each capability has one accountable owning team.

## Capabilities

- **Register Customer**: Create and maintain a customer's identity, addresses and contact preferences. Owner: Customer Services.
- **Record Life Events**: Record births, deaths and changes of circumstance for a person. Owner: Customer Services.
- **Process Employer Returns**: Receive each employer's pay and deductions return for every pay period. Owner: PAYE Operations.
- **Issue Tax Code**: Decide and issue a tax code for each employment. Owner: PAYE Operations.
- **Calculate PAYE**: Calculate the income tax due for an employment and pay period. Owner: PAYE Operations.
- **Allocate NI Number**: Allocate a National Insurance number to a person. Owner: NI Operations.
- **Calculate NI Contributions**: Calculate Class 1 contributions from the earnings in each employer return. Owner: NI Operations.
- **Maintain NI Record**: Keep each person's contribution record and qualifying years. Owner: NI Operations.
- **Open Childcare Account**: Open an account for a parent to pay a childcare provider. Owner: Family Support.
- **Check Childcare Eligibility**: Check a parent's eligibility, including income limits, every three months. Owner: Family Support.
- **Calculate Childcare Top-up**: Add the government top-up to each parent payment. Owner: Family Support.
