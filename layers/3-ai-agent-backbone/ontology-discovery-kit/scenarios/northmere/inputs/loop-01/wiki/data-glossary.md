# Data Glossary (wiki export)

Exported 2024-11-04 from the Data Office wiki.

- **Customer**: A person who has a tax, National Insurance or childcare relationship with the agency. Synonyms: Taxpayer, Citizen, Person.
- **National Insurance Number**: The lifelong reference that identifies a person for National Insurance. Synonyms: NINO.
- **Employer**: An organisation that pays employees and files PAYE returns.
- **Employment**: A period during which a person is paid by an employer.
- **Pay Period**: One period of pay, such as a week or month, reported in an employer return.
- **Tax Code**: A code that tells the employer how much tax-free pay to allow for an employment.
- **NI Contribution**: An amount of National Insurance due for a pay period.
- **NI Record**: A person's yearly contribution history, including qualifying years.
- **Childcare Account**: An account a parent uses to pay a registered childcare provider.
- **Childcare Provider**: A registered nursery, childminder or club that is paid from a childcare account.
- **Child**: A child named on a childcare account.
- **Top-up Payment**: The government contribution added to a parent's payment into a childcare account.
