# Business Glossary (wiki export)

Exported 2024-09-02 from the Operations wiki. Page owner: Data Governance.

- **Customer**: A party that holds or has applied for a product with the bank. Synonyms: Client, Account Holder.
- **Party**: A person or organisation the bank has a relationship with.
- **Customer Number**: The unique identifier for a customer, issued by ONB at application time. Synonyms: Customer ID, CIF.
- **Account**: A record of balances and transactions held for one or more customers in CoreLedger.
- **Current Account**: A product that provides day-to-day banking with a debit card and an overdraft option.
- **Payment**: An instruction to move money from a debit account to a beneficiary.
- **Mandate**: A standing authority for a third party to collect payments from an account.
- **Product Variant**: A particular configuration of a product, such as a Current Account with a premium fee tier.
