# Group Risk Management Policy (extract)

Version 2.0 · April 2024 · Group Risk

## Scope

Applies to every business unit and legal entity in the group.
RiskReg (also called the GRC platform) holds the risk register.
Each risk is recorded against the cost centre that owns it.

## Rules

- Every risk must have an owning cost centre.
- Every risk rated High must have at least one preventive control.
- Every control must be tested at least once a year.

## Classifications

Risk rating values: Low, Medium, High.

Control type values: Preventive, Detective.
