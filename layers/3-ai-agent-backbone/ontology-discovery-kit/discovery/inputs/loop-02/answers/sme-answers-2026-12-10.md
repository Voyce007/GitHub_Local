# SME answers to discovery loop 1 questions

Recorded 2026-12-10 in a working session with the Sales Operations lead, the Group Financial Controller and the Head of Enterprise Architecture.

## Customer

Customer type values: Individual, Sole Trader, Company.

The Customer Number is issued by CustHub. LedgerOne receives it through CUST-FIN-01 and stores it as the debtor reference.

## Orders

Order status values: Draft, Submitted, Confirmed, Fulfilled, Cancelled.

The 2019 order-to-cash overview pre-dates the 2023 order management upgrade and should be archived.

## Systems

TaxEngine (also called tax-engine) calculates sales tax for every invoice line.
